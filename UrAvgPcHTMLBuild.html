Essential Tools

Before you touch a single part, grab these basics:

  • Phillips (crosshead) screwdriver
  • Antistatic wrist strap (or just touch a bare metal surface often)
  • A clean, flat table with good lighting
  • Small tray or cup to hold loose screws
  • Zip ties for cable management
  • Your patience and maybe some music

Step by Step Instructions

  1. Install the CPU onto the motherboard (line up the little gold triangle).
  2. Snap the RAM sticks into their slots until they click.
  3. Install your M.2 SSD into its slot on the motherboard.
  4. Screw the motherboard into the case using the included standoffs.
  5. Mount the power supply (PSU) into the case.
  6. Install the graphics card (GPU) into the top PCIe slot.
  7. Connect all power and data cables (follow your motherboard manual).
  8. Install any extra case fans for airflow.
  9. Tidy up your cables with zip ties so air can flow freely.
  10. Double-check every connection, then power it on!

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to buy a Windows license separately?

Yes, unless you plan to install a free operating system like Linux.

What if my PC doesn't turn on the first time?

Don't panic! Double check that the power switch on your PSU is on and that the 24 pin and CPU power cables are fully seated.

Do I really need thermal paste?

Most CPU coolers come with thermal paste preapplied. If yours doesn't, a small pea sized dot in the center of the CPU is all you need.

Can I upgrade my build later?

Absolutely! That's one of the best parts of building your own PC, you can swap the GPU, add more RAM, or add more storage anytime.
